# Switzer

Switzer is designed by Jérémie Schellaert and published by the Indian Type Foundry (ITF).
It is free for personal and commercial use under the **ITF Free Font License** and is
distributed through Fontshare: <https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer>; licence terms:
<https://www.fontshare.com/terms>.

The files here are the woff2 files Fontshare serves (weights 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700,
upright only), self-hosted so the app never loads fonts from a CDN at runtime. They were
downloaded unmodified from the URLs listed by
`https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=switzer@300,400,500,600,700&display=swap`.
The @font-face rules are in `src/styles/fonts.css`.

property. Do not sell the font files on their own.
